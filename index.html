Preserving Our Heritage Bảo tồn di sản

Từ vựng

heritage
(n)
di sản
We must protect our cultural heritage for future generations.
preserve
(v)
bảo tồn
They are trying to preserve the ancient paintings in the cave.
restore
(v)
trùng tu, khôi phục
The government spent millions to restore the old temple after the flood.
landscape
(n)
phong cảnh
The beautiful landscape of the valley attracts many photographers.
folk
(adj)
dân gian
My grandmother often sings folk songs while doing housework.
historic
(adj)
có tính lịch sử quan trọng
Independence Palace is a historic site in Ho Chi Minh City.
ancient
(adj)
cổ đại
Archaeologists discovered ancient ruins buried under the sand.

Collocations

preserve cultural heritage
bảo tồn di sản văn hóa
It is everyone's duty to preserve cultural heritage.
restore a historic building
trùng tu một tòa nhà lịch sử
They used traditional materials to restore the historic building.
promote traditional values
quảng bá các giá trị truyền thống
The festival aims to promote traditional values to young people.
natural landscape
phong cảnh thiên nhiên
We should not build factories that spoil the natural landscape.

(v)
theo đuổi
He decided to pursue a career in graphic design.
apprenticeship
(n)
học nghề
An apprenticeship allows you to earn money while you learn a trade.
academic
(adj)
thuộc học thuật
She excels in academic subjects like Math and Literature.
vocational
(adj)
thuộc dạy nghề
The college offers vocational courses in mechanics and cooking.
qualification
(n)

admission
(n)
sự nhập học

pursue higher education

gain practical skills

earn a degree
đạt được bằng cấp
It usually takes four years to earn a degree in engineering.
meet admission requirements
đáp ứng yêu cầu đầu vào

awareness
(n)
nhận thức

(n)

(n)

endangered

conservation
(n)

(n)

combat climate change

Planting more trees is an effective way to combat climate change.
